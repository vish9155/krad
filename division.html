Krad Global General Trading LLC, based in Dubai with International Offices in China, USA, Italy, and India, is a trusted Global Partner for Building, Construction, Industrial, Engineering, Oil & Gas, Water, and Infrastructure Material supply. We combine Global Reach with Technical Expertise to deliver reliable, compliant, and cost-efficient solutions to Contracting Companies and Industrial Clients. We operate through Three focused Divisions, each designed to support the complex needs of Contractors, Industries, and Infrastructure Projects across the UAE and international markets.

IMPORT, STOCK & EXPORT DIVISION:

This division manages our core trading activity. We import selected Building, Construction and Industrial Consumables and Products, maintain stock in the UAE, and supply to both local and overseas clients.

This division ensures product availability, fast delivery, and competitive pricing for urgent and project-based requirements.

SOURCING AND FORWARDING CONSULTANCY DIVISION:

Our SECOND DIVISION is the BACKBONE of our GLOBAL SOURCING STRENGTH. We work as a SPECIALIZED PROCUREMENT PARTNER for contracting companies, providing safe, controlled, and fully managed international sourcing. With a strong

operational base in China, our team handles:

  • Identification of the right manufacturers and Product Opinions.
  • Negotiation for best prices and terms.
  • Complete quality inspections and compliance checks.
  • Monitoring of productions, documentations, payment, logistics and delivery.
  • A transparent process where orders are placed directly by the Contracting Company to the Manufacturer.
