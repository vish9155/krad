Strategic Industrial Supply Partner

Delivering Certainty to Critical Projects

KRAD Global General Trading LLC is a Dubai-based industrial supply and sourcing partner supporting energy, infrastructure, and industrial projects across the Middle East and international markets.

We operate at the intersection of technical compliance, commercial discipline, and logistics execution — ensuring that materials reach project sites on time, aligned with specifications, and without disruption.

From engineered piping systems to structural steel and industrial consumables, KRAD delivers with precision and accountability.

Who We Are

KRAD Global is a professionally managed industrial supply company headquartered in Dubai, UAE — one of the world’s leading trade and logistics hubs.

We support EPC contractors, industrial operators, and project owners across sectors including:

  • Oil & Gas
  • Petrochemical & Energy
  • Power & Utilities
  • Infrastructure & Industrial Construction

Our focus extends beyond supply. We integrate sourcing strategy, vendor evaluation, compliance review, and logistics coordination to function as a true project partner.
