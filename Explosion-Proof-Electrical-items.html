Explosion-Proof Electrical Items

KRAD Global General Trading LLC offers a complete range of electrical products and explosion-proof electric equipment for use in hazardous and industrial environments, including junction boxes, distribution boxes, LED light fixtures, electrical cables, and explosion-proof heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems.

All equipment provided has been chosen according to strict safety, performance, and compliance specifications when working in a high-risk environment and contributes to reducing the risk of operations, enhancing workplace safety, and providing uninterrupted industrial operations.

We provide equipment using reliable sourcing, appropriate certification, and delivery according to both project-specific and regulatory requirements.
