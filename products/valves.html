Industrial Valves Suppliers in UAE

KRAD Global General Trading LLC has built up an impressive reputation as a reputable importer, exporter, supplier, and stockholder of industrial valves located in Dubai and throughout the UAE. We offer extensive support to the oil and gas, petrochemical, marine, and power generation industries, as well as infrastructure projects, by providing quality valve solutions that conform to international quality & safety standards. We maintain a high level of stock availability due to our strong sourcing network and help our clients achieve efficient operation and timely completion of their projects through quality valve solutions. We have access to a full range of valves manufactured in carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex, and special alloys from worldwide recognized manufacturers. All of our products come with appropriate certifications to provide our customers with confidence in their suitability in environments subject to high pressures, high temperatures, and chemicals.

Our Range of Valves

We have a comprehensive range of manual and automated valves that can be used in many different industries, including

  • Motor-operated valves
  • Gas-operated valves
  • Shut-off and emergency shutdown valves (SDV & ESDV)
  • Safety and control valves (SSV and related systems)

These solutions provide efficiency, protection, and safety for flow control and systems.

Industrial Applications for Valves:

Our industrial valves can be found in many areas, including but not limited to:

  • Upstream and Downstream Oil and Gas
  • Refinery and Petrochemical Facilities
  • Marine and Offshore Facilities
  • Electric Power Generation Projects
  • Pipeline and Transmission Systems
  • Desalination, Water, and Utilities
  • Shipbuilding and Heavy Industry

This broad range allows us to meet both standard and highly customized project requirements

Quality Assurance & Sourcing Excellence

Valves are essential components of your system, and without proper valving, you run grave risks to your system, your equipment, your workers, and the environment.

To that end, we only work with approved suppliers that have strict quality control methods in place when supplying valves to us. Each supplied valve is subject to verification for material grade, pressure rating, dimension, and compliance with the relevant performance standards (e.g., ANSI or API).

In addition, our technical people have the expertise to assist customers with valve selection based on operating conditions, type of media, and project specifications.

Regional Presence & Client Support

KRAD Global General Trading LLC has extensive operations throughout the UAE, East and South Africa, and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC). Our customers are EPC

contractors, fabricators, drilling contractors, offshore engineering firms, utility companies, and industrial operators. We strive for rapid response times, competitive prices, and reliable delivery of logistics assistance to our clients.

Our Commitment

We will provide clients with reliable access to products, clear communication, and flexible solutions that enable them to meet their operational efficiency and long-term reliability.
