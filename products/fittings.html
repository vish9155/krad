Pipe Fittings Suppliers in UAE

KRAD Global General Trading LLChas established itself as a reliable supplier and exporter of premium pipe & tube fittings in the UAE for customers within the oil & gas, petrochemical, marine, energy, construction & heavy industries. Our aim is to deliver consistent, dependable fitment solutions that comply with worldwide prescribed specifications that can be utilised for all projects in the UAE, GCC, and Africa. A vast selection of products are available from our company, including seamless fittings, butt welds, forged fittings and outlet fittings, all manufactured from high-grade materials, including carbon steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, duplex steel, super duplex steel and premium nickel alloys, including Inconel, Incoloy, Hastelloy & Monel. All our products are engineered to operate effectively under high-stress situations, even in highly corrosive applications.

Applications

Pipe fittings have a broad range of applications, including:

  • O&G Upstream and Downstream
  • Refineries and Petrochemical Plants
  • Marine & Shipbuilding
  • Power & Pipeline Projects
  • Desalination, Water & Utility Systems
  • Heavy Industrial Applications

Quality & Compliance

All items offered for sale in our pipe fitting inventory are manufactured under recognised specifications from the following organisations: API, ASTM, ASME, ISO, DIN, EN, etc. We will supply proper records, certified materials, and a Certificate of Quality to accompany all shipments.

Market Presence & Clientele

KRAD Global General Trading LLC provides service to customers in the UAE, across the Middle East, and in Africa (primarily in the GCC). Customers include companies that fabricate pipes, drilling contractors, offshore engineering companies, marine engineering companies, shipyards, EPC contractors, and many other industrial businesses.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide quality, timely piping solutions to our customers by continuing to build relationships with major suppliers, offer competitive pricing, and develop long-term relationships with our customers.
