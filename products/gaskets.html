Industrial Gaskets Suppliers in UAE

KRAD Global General Trading LLC trades and sells industrial gaskets in Dubai and the UAE as a reputable importer, exporter, supplier, and stockist of gaskets. We meet the most stringent requirements for sealing applications for projects that are critical in oil & gas, petrochemicals, marine, power, and infrastructure by providing quality gaskets that seal out and keep leaks while enhancing the efficiency of your system and ensuring safe operation.

Our extensive global sourcing and our large stock on hand allow us to be a reliable partner for our customers by assuring them that they will have uninterrupted operations and that they will continue to meet the highest level of technical and safety standards. All of our gasket products come from approved manufacturers; they are all supplied with valid certification and documentation.

Quality Assurance & Supply Reliability

The function of fasteners is vital to the structural integrity and safety of equipment. For this reason, we source from reputable manufacturers that adhere to rigorous quality control standards. fasteners that are supplied will be verified for dimensional accuracy, material grade, mechanical strength, and conformance with applicable international standards.

Additionally, our expert team assists clients with fastener selection based on operation and loading condition, as well as environmental considerations.

Customer Base & Regional Reach

As a leading supplier to a wide range of customers in the Gulf Cooperative Council (GCC) and Africa, we provide products to domestic and international customers who are EPC contractors, fabricators, drilling companies, engineering consultants, utility providers, and industrial users. Due to our fast turnaround time, value for money, and efficient fleet, we have earned a reputation as the preferred supplier.
