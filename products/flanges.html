Flanges Suppliers in UAE

KRAD Global General Trading LLC is a reputable stockist, supplier, exporter, and importer of industrial-grade flanges in the UAE, with extensive experience as a source of supply to many major infrastructure, oil & gas, and industrial projects around the region. We have strong sourcing relationships, combined with the availability of prepackaged inventory, to ensure that our customers receive their flange products at the correct time and without sacrificing the quality or compliance of the products supplied

Our extensive selection of flanges includes various sizes, pressure classes, and material grades, all of which have been specifically designated for use in various operational and environmental applications. Whether utilized on high-pressure pipelines, refineries, marine installations, or within utility networks, our flanges are sourced from reputable global manufacturers and provide the necessary documentation and certification.

Materials & Technical Standards

Flanges are available in multiple grades of materials, which can withstand the rigors of demanding industrial applications. Every item is manufactured to worldwide accepted specifications (e.g., ANSI, DIN, API), thus ensuring compatibility; safety; and dependability, over extended time, in high-stress situations.

Quality Assurance & Reliability

Quality assurance is a key aspect of our manufacturing process. Each flange is thoroughly examined at the incoming hexagon and purchased from prequalified suppliers who utilize high-level quality control processes. We also provide our customers with complete material traceability, certified test results, and exact dimensions of each flange, helping our customers reduce their risk and limit the time their production will be offline.

Industrial Applications

Our flanges are used in the following sectors:

  • Oil and gas upstream & downstream projects
  • Refineries and petrochemical plants
  • Marine and offshore plants
  • Power generation plants
  • Pipeline & transmission systems
  • Desalination, water, and utilities
  • Shipbuilding/heavy industry

Each of the above requires precision, strength, and compliance. Our supply chain has been built to provide all of these elements.

Market Presence & Client Support

Our large coverage area encompasses the UAE, the Middle East, the GCC, and Africa. Our specialists are available to assist all types of fabricators, drilling contractors, offshore engineering companies, shipyards, EPCs, and industrial users. As a result of their experience in project scheduling, technical specifications, and purchasing issues, our staff can provide timely and reliable assistance during every step of the process.

Our Commitment

KRAD Global General Trading LLC supplies more than just flanges; we want long-lasting relationships with our customers by providing value through fair pricing, honesty in communications, providing technical assistance, and having a steady supply of products. The goal is to assist customers with the successful completion of their projects on time, safely, and without exceeding the budget.
