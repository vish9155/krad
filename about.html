KRAD Global General Trading LLC – Global Industrial Supply Partner.

KRAD Global General Trading LLC is the leading supplier and distributor of a comprehensive portfolio of Engineering, Industrial and Construction products. Our offered products include Steel Pipes, Valves, Fittings, Flanges, Gaskets, Fasteners, Casing & Tubing, Scaffolding, Structural Steel, Marine Products, Construction Products, Safety Products, Tools and Machines, General Hardware Products, Electrical & Instruments Products. Headquartered in Dubai, we cater to a variety of industries that include but are not limited to Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Power, Water, and construction industries. .

Krad Global functions with two major trading divisions: the Oil and Gas Division and the Construction Division. Each division is fully engaged in the delivery of specialized products and services that comply with the demands of each unique industry. The Oil and Gas Division will source, trade, and distribute energy resources, while the Construction Division provides materials, equipment, and expertise to meet construction needs in various industries. Together, these divisions allow KRAD to maintain a diversified portfolio and meet the diverse needs of different markets.

We take pride in delivering high-quality products from approved manufacturers all over the world. Our strategic relationships with these manufacturers offer us superior pricing and quality controls, which give us a competitive advantage.

We, at Krad Global, have made a name for ourselves in the industry due to our commitment to excellence in quality, delivery, and prices. We are not just suppliers; we are able to work with the EPC companies, the end users, and the traders because of the high quality of our personnel and their experience, and thereby provide the clients with the best solution for their needs.
