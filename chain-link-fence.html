Chain Link Fence System / Wire Mesh / Different Types of Fence

KRAD Global has developed a patented high-security chain-link fencing system that is designed to withstand extreme conditions. This product is manufactured using only the highest quality materials and cutting-edge production processes, thus providing the end user with unmatched durability, strength, and long-lasting service.

This fence system comes with all necessary accessories and materials so that a complete and dependable security fence is provided for high-security and restricted-use environments.

Chain-link fence systems from KRAD Global can be used for a variety of different applications, including industrial complexes, construction sites, warehouses, commercial buildings, or any perimeter security application. They are also quick to install, require little ongoing maintenance, and maintain their function regardless of environmental conditions.

  1. Chain Link Mesh:

    • Material:High-carbon steel wire

    • Wire Diameter: 4 mm

    • Mesh Size:50.8 mm x 50.8 mm

    • Height: 2.4 meters

    • Galvanization:Heavy galvanized

    • Compliance: ASTM A 392 Class 2

    • Zinc Coating:610 grams per square meter (g/m²)

  2. Posts and Supports:

    • Line/Corner Post Dimensions: 90 mm x 80 mm x 6 mm

    • Material: Heavy-duty galvanized steel

    • Footings: Concrete footings as required for stability

  3. Barbed Wire:

    • Number of Rows: 6 rows

    • Material: Galvanized steel

    • Design: Securely attached for effective deterrence

  4. Concertina Barbed Wire:

    • Number of Rows: 1 rows

    • Material: Galvanized steel

    • Design:Provides additional security and barrier
