Steel Plate and Structural Material

The Steel Plate and Structural Material division at KRAD Global is your one source for premium quality steel plates and structural materials for an extensive range of industries. The products that we provide include premium carbon steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, aluminum, brass, and bronze materials.

KRAD Global is committed to providing the highest level of quality, reliability, and customer service. All steel materials supplied by KRAD Global will meet both your project requirements and the industry standards. Summary:

  • Carbon Steel:

    Widely used due to its strength and affordability. Common in beams, channels, angles, bars, and hollow sections.

  • Stainless Steel:

    Chosen for its corrosion resistance and strength in harsh environments. Used in various structural components.

  • Aluminum:

    Valued for its lightweight and corrosion resistance. Used in applications where reduced weight is crucial.

  • Brass and Bronze:

    Less common in structural applications but useful for specific needs like corrosion resistance and machinability.

  • Alloy Steel:

    Used where enhanced mechanical properties are needed for demanding applications.

  • SS400, 4140, 1137:

    Specific grades with applications in structural and heavy-duty components.
